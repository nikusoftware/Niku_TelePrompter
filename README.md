# Niku Teleprompter

An intelligent, adaptive teleprompter application that leverages artificial intelligence to deliver a seamless, natural presentation experience. Designed for content creators, public speakers, educators, and broadcasters.

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Voice & Speech Tracking

* **Speech-Driven Auto-Scrolling:** Tracks your spoken words in real time using speech recognition and scrolls the text automatically at your exact natural speaking cadence.
* **Pause & Resume Detection:** Pauses scrolling when you stop speaking, clear your throat, or take a drink, and resumes seamlessly when you start again.
* **Ad-Lib & Deviation Handling:** Detects when you go off-script or rephrase a line, waiting until you return to the script without jumping erratically.
* **Pacing Analytics:** Provides real-time feedback on your words-per-minute (WPM) rate to help maintain ideal pacing.

### 2. AI Script Generation & Editing

* **Built-in Script Assistant:** Prompt the integrated AI to draft scripts, speeches, or video outlines from scratch.
* **Tone & Length Adjusters:** Rewrite existing scripts with a single click to alter tone (e.g., professional, casual, persuasive) or adjust length (expand or condense).
* **Automated Formatting:** Automatically generates readable line breaks, highlights key emphasis points, and formats lists for effortless reading.

### 3. Display & Layout Controls

* **Mirroring Mode:** Horizontal and vertical flipping support for physical teleprompter glass rigs.
* **Overlay & Float Mode:** Compact, semi-transparent floating window that sits directly below your camera lens for eye-contact alignment during Zoom, Teams, or recorded videos.
* **Custom Typography & Visuals:** Customizable font sizes, line heights, background colors, and margin spacing.
* **Focus Box & Reading Line:** Visual cue overlay to help keep your eyes anchored near the camera line.

### 4. Smart Video & Audio Recording

* **Built-in Recording:** Capture high-definition video while reading directly from the app.
* **AI Eye-Contact Correction:** Post-processing alignment option to keep your eyes looking directly at the camera lens at all times.
* **Automated Subtitles:** Export videos with AI-generated closed captions synchronized to your final recording.

## Quick Start Guide

1. **Import or Draft Script:** Paste your script into the text editor, or click **Generate with AI** to create a fresh script from a prompt.
2. **Configure Display:** Set font size, scrolling speed, or switch to **Speech Tracking Mode**.
3. **Select Layout:** Toggle **Mirroring Mode** if using teleprompter glass, or position the **Floating Window** near your webcam.
4. **Start Session:** Click **Prompt** or **Record** to begin.
